\chapter{Implementation Evidence}
\label{ch:implementation-evidence}

This chapter presents the evidence of the physical and functional
implementation of the gantry crane system. Evidence is provided
for each of the five subsystems, namely EERI I, EERI II, MCTR I,
MCTR II, and REII, followed by evidence of the integrated system.
The purpose is to demonstrate how the design was realised through
fabrication, assembly, electrical integration, and software
implementation, where applicable.

Photographs, engineering drawings, circuit diagrams, software
listings, and other relevant artefacts are included to document
the implementation. Each item of evidence is linked to the
corresponding subsystem or design requirement wherever possible.

\section{EERI I Implementation Evidence}
\label{sec:eeri-i-evidence}

This section documents the implementation of EERI I and provides
evidence of the completed work. The evidence should demonstrate
the physical construction, electrical or mechanical connections,
and other implementation details relevant to the subsystem.

\subsection{Physical Implementation}

Figure~\ref{fig:eeri-i-implementation1} presents the physical implementation of a single phase of the inverter using 2 
IGBT switches and a driver IC. The connection was done on a breadboard and the IGBT legs were soldered to connecter leads.

\begin{figure}[H]
    \centering
    \includegraphics[width=0.8\textwidth]
    {figures/Single_phase_test}
    \caption{Single phase breadboard ciruit.}
    \label{fig:eeri-i-implementation1}
\end{figure}

Figure \ref{fig:eeri-i-implementation1} show the single phase connection between the dirver IC and the 2
IGBTs. A low voltage supplied by the benchtop DC power supply was used for this test. The physical breadboard
implementation of the circuit helped identify several misconseptions and future adaptations that would have to
be made to realise the final circuit. The results of this low voltage test are given in Figure \ref{fig:eeri-i-implementation2}.

\begin{figure}[H]
    \centering
    \includegraphics[width=0.8\textwidth]
    {figures/Single_Phase_response}
    \caption{Single Phase IGBT Switching.}
    \label{fig:eeri-i-implementation2}
\end{figure}

As seen in Figure \ref{fig:eeri-i-implementation2} the IGBT is swithced using a square wave generated by the signal 
generator which as a duty cycle of 60\%. A low DC voltage of 10V is switched over a resistive load of about 
$100\,\mathrm{k}\Omega$. The 4N25 optocoupler was tested seperately, the test results are given in Figure 
\ref{fig:eeri-i-implementation3}.

\begin{figure}[H]
    \centering
    \includegraphics[width=0.8\textwidth]
    {figures/Opto_results}
    \caption{Optocouper test results}
    \label{fig:eeri-i-implementation3}
\end{figure}

The test was conducted using a 50\% duty cycle 10kHz square wave, the blue trace represents the output on the load side of the
optocoupler.

REII hardware testing and software development.
Implementation of the final ESP32-S3 crane system remains
incomplete.

\subsection{Physical Implementation}

Figure~\ref{fig:reii-implementation} shows the earlier radio
bench comprising two original ESP32 boards and two nRF24
modules. Later bench development used the STM32F411 board
as the ground station, with UART communication to the PC
and a radio link to the original ESP32 controller.
Figure~\ref{fig:reii-ground-station} shows the ground-station
board, programmer and radio wiring. The receiver firmware
generated physical PWM outputs while motion and sensor
readings were simulated. The current controller is the
university-supplied ESP32-S3 board, which still requires
firmware migration and crane integration.

which the design has been implemented. The implementation records
