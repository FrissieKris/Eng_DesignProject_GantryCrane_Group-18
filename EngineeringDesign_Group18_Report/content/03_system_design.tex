\subsection{EERI II: Magnetic Load Attachment: F/U 1.2}
\label{subsec:eeri2-fu1p2}
The EERI II subsystem, engineered by J.P. Smith,
provides electromagnetic payload retention,
fast power switching and analog current sensing.
The subsystem decomposes into four lower level functional units (Table~).\ref{tab:functional-units-eeri2}.

\begin{table}[H]
    \centering
    \caption{EERI II Operational Functional Units.}
    \label{tab:eeri2-functional-units}
    \begin{tabular}{@{} l l p{8.5cm} @{}}
        \toprule
        \textbf{F/U ID} & \textbf{Name} & \textbf{Description} \\
        \midrule
        F/U 1.2.1 & Magnetic Circuit \& Coils & Three-point U-core electromagnet assembly incorporating six custom bobbins ($28\,\text{m}$ of $1.0\,\text{mm}$ OD enamelled copper wire each, 286~turns/spool) providing $\ge 268.7\,\text{N}$ static holding capacity on $\ge 5\,\text{mm}$ steel plates. \\
        F/U 1.2.2 & Power Switching \& Gate Drive & High-current low-side switching stage using an IRF540Z power MOSFET driven by a TC4420 6\,A non-inverting driver from the $+13.88\,\text{V}$ rail, protected by an MBR1060 Schottky flyback diode. \\
        F/U 1.2.3 & Current Sensing \& Conditioning & Galvanically isolated COTS ACS712-30A Hall-effect module conditioned via dual LM358 op-amp differential gain and offset subtraction, scaling $0\text{--}3\,\text{A}$ to $0.015\text{--}1.980\,\text{V} \le 2.048\,\text{V}$. \\
        \bottomrule
    \end{tabular}
\end{table}
