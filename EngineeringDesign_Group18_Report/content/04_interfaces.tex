\subsection{EERI I:  }
\label{subsec:eeri1-interfaces}
EERI I Drive Electroncics by Jopie Roux.

Table \ref{tab1:interfaces-eeri1} and \ref{tab2:interfaces-eeri1} define the the external inteerfaces that 
cross the boundary of the drive electronics (F/U 1.1) and the internal interfaces bewtween
its level 2 functional units. The interface type letters correspond to figure \ref{fig:arch-eeri1}, where E denotes
electrical interfaces, S signal interfaces and M mechanical interfaces. 
The drive-electronics subsystem receives single-phase AC, distributes protected power to the 
required PSUs, receives control signals from REII, and supplies the three-phase induction 
motor used by MCTR II. It also returns conditioned current feedback to the controller and provides local 
hardware protection of the inverter.

    \centering

    \label{tab1:interfaces-eeri1}

    \begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{} l l l X @{}}
        \toprule
        \textbf{Interface} & \textbf{From} & \textbf{To} & \textbf{Description} \\
        \midrule

        I/F 1.0 E & F/U 2.0 & F/U 1.1.1 &
        Single-phase 230 VAC, 50 Hz mains input to the drive electronics. The input is routed through the required main switching and protection using 15 A trip switches before being distributed to the rest of the system. \\

        I/F 1.2 E & F/U 1.1.1 & F/U 1.2 &
        Protected mains supply to the 12 VDC power supply. \\

        I/F 1.6 E & F/U 1.1.1 & F/U 1.4 &

        I/F 1.5 S & F/U 1.1 & F/U 1.1 &
        Control and feedback interface between the REII controller and the drive electronics. The controller provides isolated PWM signals used by the driver ICs to switch the DC bus voltage.         I/F 1.7 E & F/U 1.1.5 & F/U 1.7 &
        Three-phase AC from the inverter to the 180 W induction motor. \\

        I/F 1.8 M & F/U 1.7 & F/U 1.3.1 &

\begin{table}[H]
    \centering
    \caption{Internal interfaces of EEII I (F/U 1.1, Level 2).}
    \label{tab2:interfaces-eeri1}

    \begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{} l l l X @{}}
        \toprule
        \textbf{Interface} & \textbf{From} & \textbf{To} & \textbf{Description} \\
        \midrule

        I/F 1.1.1 E & F/U 1.1.1 & F/U 1.1.2 &
        Protected single-phase 230 VAC supplied to the rectifier stage after the system protection and switching
        devices.\\

        I/F 1.1.2 E & F/U 1.1.2 & F/U 1.1.3 &
        High voltage rectified DC supplied to the DC bus for smoothing with a bulk capacitor.\\

        I/F 1.1.3 E & F/U 1.1.3 & F/U 1.1.5 &
        Smooth HV DC voltage supplied to the 6 switch inverter.\\

        I/F 1.1.4 S & F/U 1.1.4 & F/U 1.1.5 &
        Isolated PWM signals used to contorl the IGBT switching. The IR2104 gate driver ICs are used to switch
        the IGBTs using the provided PWM signal. Between the REII controller and the IR2104s a optocoupler is 
        inserted to ensure electrical isolation.\\

        I/F 1.1.5 S & F/U 1.1.5 & F/U 1.1.6 &
        Inverter operating current information sent to the sensing and protection stage using a 
        ACS72981xLR Hall-effect current sensor. This path is used to detect a overcurrent or shoot-through fault 
        and initialte a local inverter shutdown.\\

        I/F 1.1.6 E & F/U 1.2 & F/U 1.1.4 &
        12VDC used to power the driver ICs obtained from EEII II's PSU. \\

        \bottomrule
    \end{tabularx}
\end{table}

The main power path flows from the protected mains to the bridge rectifier, to the DC bus for smoothing using a bulk
capacitor to supply the 3-phase inverter. In parallel, a 12VDC PSU powers the gate driver ICs which use the isolated 
PWM singals from REII to switch the IBTSs and create the 3-phase AC for the induction motor. The EEII I subsystem
also provides a current-sense signal to REII for feedback and local protection of the inverter.

\noindent\textbf{Open interface questions raised by this table:}
(i) the mains supply to the Brake PSU, required by the system specification to be
distributed by EEII~I, does not appear on the drawings; (ii) the power and
current-sense cable of the magnetic attachment must follow the rope without
carrying tension, which is a cable interface between MCTR II and EEII~II that
is also not drawn; (iii) the drawing numbers the MCTR II sub-units 1.3.x,
which duplicates MCTR I (F/U 1.3), so they are renumbered 1.4.x here.
